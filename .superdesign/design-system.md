# Pranav portfolio

## Shared foundation
Every public page uses paper #f5f4ef, charcoal #252922, muted text #687062, sage #53634f, rules #d6d9ce, surface #fafaf5, and accent wash #e9ede2. Tokens live in src/css/styles.css; src/css/portfolio.css provides the common visual baseline and is loaded after section styles. Use Crimson Text for reading and headings, IBM Plex Mono for navigation and metadata. The page shell is 1088px, the reading column 680px, and mobile gutters 20px.

## Navigation and hierarchy
All 27 pages share the same author header and Compute, Reflect, Culture, Reach navigation. Experience and writing use a quiet sidebar and aligned reading grid. Titles share the same scale; rules, spacing, buttons, focus outlines, and footer treatment remain consistent across sections.

## Section character
Compute uses engineering diagrams, technical metadata, and restrained tables. Reflect uses the compact numbered receipt layout with inline excerpts on hover or focus; essay pages retain italic pull quotes. Culture retains its interactive favorites web and a common collection list, with thin book spines and small episode labels. Reach uses a ruled list of contact links and the existing find-me game. Preserve working media, native dialogs, actual destination URLs, and existing content. Semantic result colors and the game target can differ from the accent when needed to communicate information.

## Restraint
Use flat surfaces, fine rules, generous whitespace, and the shared sage accent. Avoid separate section palettes, paper stacks, heavy cards, gradients, moving backgrounds, and decorative glows. Individual flavor comes from content and small motifs. Honor keyboard navigation, no-JS content, and reduced motion.

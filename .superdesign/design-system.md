## Current direction
Each homepage realm has its own interface while sharing the original paper background, Crimson Text, IBM Plex Mono and restrained palette. Compute stays unchanged. Culture is a dense 20-spoke, eight-ring scalloped web, with faded ink strands, muted-red radial accents and a larger angular spider. Five sharp paper labels with red top borders sit at junctions. Hover and keyboard focus draw a traveling red thread and move the spider; reduced motion keeps it still. The web is shared by the homepage and Favorites hub at every screen size. Favorites navigation and intros are centered; every category collection and the photo gallery use a centered 640px column. Reflect is a ruled notebook of essay links using the warm words palette. Reach is a correspondence card with labeled contact links and the existing find-me game link. The favorites hub repeats the web; category pages retain their working media, summaries and native dialogs. No oversized marketing hero or unrelated content.

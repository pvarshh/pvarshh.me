01 / Context

Evaluation quality starts with the inputs.

My work addressed two parts of the evaluation workflow: preparing useful inputs for annotation and scoring the responses produced by models. Candidate selection and response measurement both affect the quality of the resulting evaluation data.

The projects brought together retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), candidate filtering, reranking, and benchmark infrastructure.

02 / Pipeline design

Retrieve, filter, and rerank.

I developed a retrieve-and-rerank RAG pipeline to automate LLM inputs for data annotation. Candidate filtering was part of that preparation, narrowing the material passed through the pipeline.

I also worked on multi-stage reranking using LLM-as-a-judge and adjudication. These stages supported the goal of reducing annotation errors.

03 / Evaluation

Make response scoring a repeatable workflow.

I built a high-throughput benchmark system that scored more than 8,000 model responses per day. The reported outcome of the evaluation work was a 60% reduction in annotation errors.

The two workstreams addressed different parts of the same problem: preparing candidate inputs and assessing the model outputs used in annotation. Treating evaluation as a pipeline made those responsibilities explicit.
